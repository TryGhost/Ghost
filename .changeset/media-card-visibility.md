Added web/email visibility settings to the image, gallery, video, audio, file and embed cards, so media can be shown in the email but hidden on the published post (and vice versa, or limited to a member segment)

Every card with settings now has a single Edit button that opens one settings panel, with Visibility as its last tab. Image, gallery and embed cards open it with Edit, a double-click or Cmd+Enter, and the visibility indicator of wide and full-width cards now sits inside the card
