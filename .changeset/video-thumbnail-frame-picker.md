Added a thumbnail picker to the video card: its custom thumbnail can now be a frame chosen from the video itself, scrubbed along a filmstrip, as well as an uploaded image. The editor's modal, also used by the Markdown help, became usable from the keyboard: keys work inside it without reaching the editor, Tab stays inside it, focus goes back to where it was when nothing else took it, its close button works with Enter and Space, and a file dropped on it no longer opens in the tab
